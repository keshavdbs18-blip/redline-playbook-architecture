# Clause taxonomy

Every extracted redline, drafted comment, and boilerplate decision must tag
against one of the categories below (adapted from the CUAD — Contract
Understanding Atticus Dataset — 41-category schema). If nothing fits, tag
`unclassified` and say why in one sentence; do not stretch an existing
category to cover it.

## Substantive (high redline-variance expected)

- Indemnification
- Limitation of Liability / Cap on Liability
- IP Ownership & Assignment
- License Grant & Scope
- Termination for Convenience / for Cause
- Non-Compete / Exclusivity
- Most Favored Nation
- Change of Control
- Governing Law / Venue
- Audit Rights
- Insurance
- Data Protection / Confidentiality
- Warranty
- Payment Terms / Late Fees
- Anti-Assignment
- Renewal / Auto-Renewal

## Usually boilerplate (see `boilerplate-list` for the suppression rule)

- Severability
- Notices
- Counterparts
- Entire Agreement
- Force Majeure
- Headings / Interpretation
- Waiver

This list is a starting point, not a ceiling — extend it as your own
corpus surfaces clause types that don't fit, but keep every addition here
so the taxonomy stays the single source of truth every agent reads from.
